robin-klipp-verktyg

A desktop tool for a creator to post short gaming clips to TikTok. The creator connects a TikTok account, reviews each clip, edits the caption and hashtags, chooses who can view it and which interactions are allowed, and then publishes it. Nothing is sent to TikTok until the creator presses Publish.
